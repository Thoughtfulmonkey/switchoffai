# Steam

The game marketplace and distribution platform used by both indie developers and large publishers.

## Using SteamDB to avoid AI

Steam requires publishers to label games that include AI generated content, and to explain how AI was used. This disclosure is shown in the game's description on its Steam store page.

However, you can't use the AI content disclosure to filter games on the Steam site. For that, you'll need to use an independent cataloging site: SteamDB.

1. Go to [https://steamdb.info/instantsearch/](https://steamdb.info/instantsearch/).
1. In the **Search tags** field, enter `ai`.
1. Hover over the **AI Content Disclosed** tag, and then select **exclude**.

## Ignoring publishers and developers

If you aren't interested in games from a particular publisher or developer, because of their use of generative AI or any other reason, you can choose to ignore them on Steam. This stops their games from being recommended to you.

To ignore a publisher or developer, complete the following steps:

1. Open Steam.
1. Select the **STORE** tab.
1. Search for a game from the publisher or developer.
1. On the game's store page, select the publisher or developer.
1. On the publisher or developer page, select the gear icon (Options), and then select **Ignore this creator**.
