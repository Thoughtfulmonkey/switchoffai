# Instagram

Instagram is a social media site from Meta that focuses on photos and short videos.

## Withdrawing consent for Muse Image

[Muse Image](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/introducing-muse-image-meta-ai/) is an AI-powered image generation service that's available on Instagram. By default, the images that you upload to Instagram can be fed into Muse Image by other Instagram users. To withdraw your consent and prevent other users from creating AI-generated derivatives from your images, complete the following steps:

1. Open the Instagram app. You must use the Android or iOS app. You can't access the required settings on the Instagram website.
1. Tap your profile icon.
1. On your profile page, tap the three horizontal bars icon in the upper-right corner.
1. On the **Settings and activity** page, scroll down and tap **Sharing and reuse**.
1. On the **Sharing and reuse** page, scroll down to the **Allow people to reuse your content on Instagram and with AI features at Meta** section.
1. Tap the toggles for **Posts** and **Reels** to turn off permission. The permission is turned off when the circle is on the left.
1. **Optional**: Check that permission in the **Allow people to create with and reuse your original audio on Meta AI** is also turned off.
