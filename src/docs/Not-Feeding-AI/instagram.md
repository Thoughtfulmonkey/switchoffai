# Instagram

Instagram is a social media site from Meta that focuses on photos and short videos.

!!! note "Update about Muse Image" 
    [Muse Image](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/introducing-muse-image-meta-ai/) is an AI-powered image generation service that's available on Instagram. When it was first released, the default permissions allowed anyone to feed your Instagram images into the AI generator. In response to public outcry, Meta removed that ability three days after release. However, the permission setting to allow other people to use your audio with Meta AI is still there, and turned on by default.

## Withdrawing consent to use your audio for Meta AI
To withdraw your consent and prevent other users from creating AI-generated derivatives from your audio, complete the following steps:

1. Open the Instagram app. You must use the Android or iOS app. You can't access the required settings on the Instagram website.
1. Tap your profile icon.
1. On your profile page, tap the three horizontal bars icon in the upper-right corner.
1. On the **Settings and activity** page, scroll down and tap **Sharing and reuse**.
1. On the **Sharing and reuse** page, scroll down to the **Allow people to create with and reuse your original audio on Meta AI** section.
1. Tap the toggle for **Reels** to turn off permission. The permission is turned off when the circle is on the left.
